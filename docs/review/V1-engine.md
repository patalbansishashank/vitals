## Verified fine

- Unit constants: `KCAL_PER_KJ` = 1/4.184, `KCAL_PER_MJ` 239.0057, `RHO.fat` 9441 kcal/kg (39.5 MJ/kg), glycogen 4.207 kcal/g (17.6 kJ/g), `MMOL_NA_PER_G` 1000/22.99. The engine is kcal-only internally, so kJ display belongs to the UI.
- Published coefficients checked against the formulas: CUN-BAE, RFM, US-Navy inch and metric forms, Jackson-Pollock 3-site (both sexes), Siri, Watson TBW, Deurenberg 1991, Gallagher 2000, Mifflin–St Jeor, Müller FFM (MJ), Cunningham 1980.
- Determinism: the same inputs gave bit-identical daily series for 9 profiles (both sexes, BMI 13 and 60, age 18 and 90, zero-intake days, wake at 00:00, horizon 1 day, sex unspecified). `simulateEnsemble` with the same seed gave identical bands. A→B→A runs were identical. The module-level state (`CHOL_FACTOR`, `cachedDefaults`, the `nominal` params, WeakMap caches keyed by profile) is caches of constants only.
- Non-finite outputs: across the probes and the model fuzz (600 h fast, 30 % of maintenance for 120 d, 250 % overfeeding, zero protein) the only NaN series are the sex-specific ones that are NaN by design (`testosterone` for women and unspecified sex, `menstrualRisk` for men and post-menopause). Fat, glycogen and fluids stayed non-negative.
- Dates: the engine uses UTC ISO arithmetic only (`T00:00:00Z`), so it has no local-time drift. Weekday Monday = 0 is consistent. The 04:00 day rollover is not in the engine (outside this area).
- Time constants: daily modules use τ in days and hourly modules use τ·24. The fasting oedema two-pool step matches its closed form.
- Macro resolution (`gPerKgBw`, `gPerKgFfm`, `pctEnergy`, `remainder`, alcohol, MCT, carbShareNonProtein) reproduces the reference energy. The refeed ramp matches the spec (50/50/75 then 100 %). Fast masks over midnight and past the horizon end are correct.
- Body: an `estimateInitialState` sweep (age 15–100, height 120–220 cm, BMI 10–70, waist absent or 40–250, known BF 0 or 70, sliders 0 or 1) gave no NaN, FM + FFM = W, and no negative depots. The fusion matrix is positive definite. `interp`, LMS age clamp 20–80, and `sliderTo` at s = 1 were checked.
- Assimilation: Kalman and RTS algebra, the 14-day reset, same-day weigh-in merge, check-in windows, `reanchor` purity, and parameter constants against their comments.
- Validation run: 177 scenarios, 601 expectations; 391 pass, 100 registered, 13 tracked, 58/97 direction-only. This is identical to `KNOWN_MISSES.md`, so the engine changes in this pass did not move any validation row.
