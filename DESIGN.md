# Design System: Vitals

## Revision r068 (2026-10-28, owner's "unslop" request)

This revision supersedes the statements below wherever they disagree; the tokens in `design/tokens.css` and the
foundation section of `src/components/README.md` (§0) are the source of truth. Revision r071 (next) supersedes parts of
this block; each such item says "superseded by r071".

- **Type (U10):** Manrope is retired. **Inter** (text, labels, keys, chips, tables, ticks) and **Figtree** (page and card
  titles, section headings, big values), both self-hosted variable fonts, latin + latin-ext. Weights 400 text · 500
  labels and keys · 600 titles and values; Inter never renders below 400, Figtree never below 600. Tabular numerals
  stay on the root. The "Three Weights Rule" and the 550 ceiling are retired. (Superseded by r071: Geist and Geist
  Mono replace Inter and Figtree.)
- **Shapes (U2, U8):** controls are rounded rectangles in the card's language, not pills: keys 10 px (8 small, 12
  large; icon-only keys stay round), selectors a 12 px well with 9 px segments 3 px inside, chips 6 px tags. The
  floating navigation pill, switches, dots and meters keep their round ends. (Superseded in part by r071: the primary
  key is a gold pill, chips are pills again and the card corner is 20 px.)
- **Selectors (U2):** every segmented control, tab list and link bank fills the width it is given, equal segments,
  the selected one the gold tile (gold wash, gold edge, ivory label). (Superseded in part by r071: the labels are mono
  caps, weight 500 in every state.)
- **Keys (U8):** `outlined` is a neutral tile (6 % ink fill, strong hairline, ivory label) so an ordinary button
  reads as one; a text key that is a card's main action (head or foot) takes the action tile.
- **Chips (U12):** quiet tags (6 % ink tint, stone label, no border); status chips are a dot and words only. (r071: a
  tag is a 22 px pill and a chip that acts is a 28 px pill.)
- **Layout (U4, U5, U11):** no page max-width anywhere; one column by default (`<Stack>`), an explicit two-column
  `<Columns>` only where it reads better. One spacing rhythm: gutter 16 / 24 px, 16 px between cards, card padding
  16 / 20 px, 12 px inside a card. (Superseded in part by r071: card padding 18 / 24 px; 12 px between cards in a card
  grid and inside a card, 16 px from 1024; 16 px between page sections.)
- **Scrollbars (U6, U7):** none on touch and narrow screens; on desktop thin theme-ink thumbs on transparent tracks,
  and the page's own bar is an overlay thumb over the backdrop (never the black native track).
- **Navigation (U14):** one stable desktop sidebar in labelled groups (Today · Food · Train · Ring / Planning /
  Library, Settings at the foot); a labelled five-key phone dock with Ring and More; a real More page for the
  places the dock has no key for; the header ⋯ for a page's own actions only. Settings is master/detail from
  1024 px. Group labels are sentence case (13/18 600), never uppercase. (r071: the dock's current key and the
  sidebar's current row are ivory, not gold.)
- **Sidebar (U13):** its key lives in the sidebar's own head (right end; the rail's top key when collapsed), not in
  the top bar.
- **Motion (U9):** Lenis smooth scrolling on desktop pointers; GSAP card entrances on page changes (10 px rise and
  fade, 420 ms, staggered). Both off under reduced motion and on touch (Lenis). (r071 adds the picture motion hooks and
  the spring: see the next revision.)

## Revision r071 (2026-10-30, the owner's UI overhaul)

This revision supersedes the r068 block above and every statement below that disagrees with it; a standing decision it
names is history. The sources are `r071/SPEC.md` (the owner's decisions), `r071/RESEARCH.md` (type, colour, surfaces) and
`r071/AUDIT.md` (the per-card plan). The tokens in `design/tokens.css` are the source of truth for every value;
`src/components/README.md` §0 and §3 and `design/COMPONENTS.md` §16 to §20 say how to use them. Package 0A (tokens, type,
material, chrome, primitives and the focus fix) and package 0B (the cards, pictures, grid and library) are built: the card
and picture components (`GradientCard`, `CardHead`, `InfoPanel`, `CardFoot`, `MonoMeta`, `HeroNumber`, `StatusDot`, the viz
set, `Rail`, `CardGrid`, `CardLibrary`) are exported from `@/components`, and the page packages put them on the pages.

The owner's brief was "unslopify, not slopify: one design we follow". The two references the owner chose in r071 (a
monochrome tasks app with mono caps and light numerals; a running app with glow cards) refine Ember: Vitals keeps its
charcoal, grain, gold and hairlines, and takes from them the mono facts, the light numerals, the 20 px cards, one soft
glow per card and an ivory "you are here". When in doubt, choose the quieter option that still reads like the references.

- **Type pair** (supersedes r068 U10, Inter and Figtree, and the Manrope text further down): Geist for text, titles and
  numerals, Geist Mono for facts. No serif (SPEC Q3); `--lm-font-title` is the one switch point. Geist is declared 300–600
  and Geist Mono 400–600, so nothing is heavier than 600; 300 exists only for numerals of 36 px and up.
- **Numerals** (supersedes "tabular numerals everywhere"): hero, wide and tile numbers (64 / 52 / 44 px, 300, −0.035em)
  are proportional; whatever changes in place, aligns in a column or reads as a clock (the 36 px time too) stays tabular.
- **Uppercase** (supersedes "the only uppercase text in the product is the Settings group label" and "don't set text in
  uppercase outside the Settings group labels"): the only uppercase text is the mono caps role (Geist Mono 500, +0.08em,
  11 px, 12 px from 1024: `--lm-text-meta`), for facts only. The uppercase sans role `--lm-text-section` is gone;
  Settings and More group labels are sentence case.
- **The Fact Line Rule** (supersedes "a line under a heading"): a fact is mono caps and never sits above a heading as a
  kicker, with one exception chosen by the owner after the r068 rules (SPEC Q4, "like the references"): the top bar
  draws the page's date, or its one fact, above the title. The title is 24/28 at 600 (SPEC Q2: no large collapsing
  title); the bar stays 56 px (66 px on a phone tab page with its fact row).

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Charcoal Instrument"**

Vitals is a precise instrument that is also humane, and its surface reads that way: a near-black, finely grained backdrop with a faint warm-gold wash from the top right, charcoal cards that carry the same grain, ivory text, stone secondary text and one warm gold that only ever means something. A screen is one header, one overflow menu, content and a floating dock. It is read in seconds on a phone (open, see today and the next thing, log it in one tap, leave) and explored for longer at a laptop, so density is calm but not sparse: 12 px between cards (16 px from 1024 px) and inside them, tabular numerals wherever a value changes in place so values never jitter, and big numbers set light and proportional.

The look is the owner's reference, Lumen Health "Ember", carried over to the web and refined in r071 by the two references the owner chose (see Revision r071): hairline gold borders drawn at 0.5 px, 20 px card corners, one gold pill for the primary key and rounded-rectangle controls otherwise, Geist for text and numerals with Geist Mono caps for facts, one soft glow in a card's domain hue where a card opts in, tintless frosted chrome. The system is flat. Depth is tone, hairline and grain, never shadow; a glow is colour inside a card, not depth. Vitals is dark only; there is one palette on `:root`, no light theme and no theme switch. The product refuses the stacked-bars, captioned-everything dashboard: no explainer subtext a label already says, nothing injected between content sections, no persistent bar that changes meaning per page.

This file supersedes `design/DESIGN_DIRECTION.md` and `design/REVIEW_FINDINGS.md`, which describe the retired v1 Ulm/Braun world (mineral-grey chassis, white faceplates, Archivo in three widths, a light theme, yellow-dot key banks). Nothing from that world is inherited. Token values live in `design/tokens.css` (master) and its byte-identical copy `src/styles/tokens.css`; `src/styles/tailwind-theme.css` maps them into Tailwind v4 and resets every default Tailwind colour, radius and shadow so an off-system value cannot be typed.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-black grained backdrop (fixed, never scrolls) with a 5.5 % gold radial wash at the top right.
- Charcoal grained cards, 0.5 px gold hairline, 20 px corners, no shadow, never nested; a card may opt into one soft glow in its domain hue.
- Gold for the primary action, the selection and the moment; data wears one of eight domain hues; ivory marks where you are.
- Geist for text, titles and numerals, Geist Mono caps for facts; tabular numerals on the root, big numbers light (300) and proportional; weights 300 (numerals of 36 px and up) / 400 / 500 / 600, nothing heavier.
- A gold pill for the primary key and rounded rectangles for every other control; one floating five-key dock on phones, a collapsible sidebar on desktop.
- One top bar on every page; tintless frost on the top bar and the dock, nowhere else.
- Dark only.

**Under 1024 px:** a frosted dock across the bottom edge holds the floating dock pill of five labelled keys (64 px tall, max 34 rem wide). Content keeps clear of it. A phone screen without the pill (the first-run intake, and every pushed screen: a pushed page shows Back and its title, never the navigation) puts its action row in the pill's place instead, as the docked flow bar: the same frosted dock holding a control pill built like the navigation pill, with the flow's keys on it (see Components). On a short page the column fills the screen so that bar sits on the bottom edge, with 24 px above it; toasts rise 12 px above it. Horizontal strips (readouts, plan cards, chips) are snap rails whose scroll padding equals the gutter and whose next cell deliberately peeks. The date control is not one: it is a single full-width row that always fits (see Date control).

**From 1024 px:** the navigation is a collapsible sidebar at the left (see Sidebar): expanded 240 px or collapsed to a 64 px icon rail, toggled by the top bar's first key and Ctrl/⌘ + B and kept per device. Until the person chooses, it is expanded from 1280 px and collapsed below. The content column is the window minus the sidebar, so a layout keyed to a window width gets 240 px less (64 px collapsed). With Evidence, Planning tools and Settings in the sidebar, the desktop ⋯ carries only the page's own actions and is absent on a page without any. Editors and explanations become 392 px side panels instead of bottom sheets; from 1280 px they dock in the flow.

Breakpoints: 480, 768 (two-column forms, 14 px inputs), 1024 (sidebar, side panels), 1280 (sidebar expanded by default, docked panels).

### Named Rules
**The One Header Rule.** One top bar, one ⋯, one banner slot per screen. No bar at the foot beside the navigation, no bar above the navigation, no persistent bar whose meaning changes per page. The one thing that docks at the foot is a phone flow's step keys, and only in the navigation pill's place on a screen that has no pill.

**The One Bar Height Rule.** Every control in the header's title row and in the action row is exactly one height: 36 px for a fine pointer, 44 px for a coarse one. That includes a key bank: its track is the bar height and its segments fill it (12 px side padding, 14 px labels, still mono caps). The bar's own keys (the sidebar key, Back and ⋯) are 48 px round targets with 24 px icons, and the status icons are 48 px round targets with 22 px icons; the bar's tabs and progress rows keep the components' own sizes.

**The No Nested Cards Rule.** A card never holds another card. A list of similar items becomes rows inside one card.

## Elevation & Depth

Vitals is flat. Every shadow token is `none`. Depth comes from three things only: tonal steps (Ember Black backdrop, Charcoal Card, Raised Charcoal for anything floating), the 0.5 px gold hairline, and grain. The grain is a seamless, fine film grain (per-pixel noise with its long wavelengths cut, so it never mottles into felt; lossless, so no compression blocks) drawn at one image pixel per device pixel at any screen scale or zoom. Each surface's tone is baked into its own tile: 72 % Ember Black over the grain on the backdrop, 83 % Charcoal Card on cards, 83 % Deep Charcoal on the desktop sidebar. That leaves each surface one CSS gradient, its gold radial wash from the top right (5.5 % on the backdrop, 3.5 % on cards, none on the sidebar), because every CSS gradient carries the browser's ordered dither and two stacked read as a woven grid. The backdrop is fixed to the viewport so the grain never scrolls, and the card grain is anchored to the viewport too, so a card edge between device pixels never resamples it. Frost is chrome, not a surface: three stacked tintless backdrop blurs (4, 9 and 14 px), each with its own mask, so the material is densest under the chrome's keys and thins toward the edge content passes under. Each layer's box reaches 32 px past the visible edge (hidden by its mask) so the blur samples real content there. The top bar's frost reaches 24 px below the bar without taking room and thins out in steps across the bar's bottom edge (the 14 px layer gone at the edge, the 9 px halfway down the band, the 4 px at its end), so content dissolves into the bar as it passes under, as in Lumen's header; its opacity follows the first 80 px of scroll, and on desktop the hairline marks the bar only at rest. The phone dock and the docked flow bar mirror the fade, all three layers fading in over 24 px above them without taking room. The welcome flow's header uses the same frost. Floating surfaces sit over a 60 % black scrim.

**r071: one glow per card.** A card may opt into ONE soft glow in its domain hue with `data-domain` (`energy`, `heart`, `steps`, `food`, `recovery`, `body`, `sleep`, `videos`; `plan` or `gold` for the Next-up card; `neutral` is the default card). The glow is a single eased radial gradient painted in OKLab (five stops, alpha 1, .84, .50, .16 and 0 at 0, 20, 42, 64 and 86 % of the ellipse) over the card's grain tile over the card colour. It takes the place of the gold wash on that card and is never a second gradient layer on top of it (two read as a woven grid); the grain keeps it from banding. It is anchored away from small text: no small stone text and no solid-coloured mark in its core, while ivory text and marks are safe everywhere (at least 5.1:1 at every glow's brightest point). It is never animated: it fades in once over 900 ms through the registered `--lm-glow-k` property (`data-glow="pending"` holds it at 0), and `--lm-glow-k` (0 to 1) scales or switches off every glow (there is no user setting, SPEC Q6). It sits behind an `@supports` test, so an engine without `in oklab` or `color-mix()` keeps the plain card. A banner takes the card material with no glow; an inset well and a control never take one. The recipe is in Components, Domain card.

### Shadow Vocabulary
None. The `box-shadow` values in the build are focus lines (an inset 1 px gold ring added to a field's border on focus, with no layout shift, and an inset 2 px focus ring on menu items), 2 px rings that keep a small mark readable (a badge dot's, the live mark's), and the hard colour extension a sheet and a side panel paint past the screen edge (0 blur, 0 spread, offscreen at rest: it only hides the gap a spring's overshoot would open). None casts a shadow and none is blurred.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Material Rule.** Surfaces never cast shadows or bevel. A pressed key changes its background or brightness, never its depth. (Superseded in part by r071: the clause "or glow" is retired. A card may carry one domain glow inside its own colour; it is colour, not depth, it never reaches outside the card and it is never animated. The live endpoint's two rings are marks, not surfaces: they pulse twice, 4.8 s, then stop.)

**The Grain Twice Rule.** Grain and the gold wash appear on two surfaces: the page backdrop and the card material. The desktop sidebar takes the grain without the wash, through 83 % bg-2. A card that opts into a domain swaps its wash for ONE glow gradient, never both: no card carries two gradient layers. (Superseded in part by r071: "no other element carries a texture or a gradient" now reads: nothing but the card's own glow adds a gradient inside a card, and no other element carries a texture or a gradient.)

**The Tintless Frost Rule.** Backdrop blur belongs to the top bar, the dock and the docked toolbar, with no tint and no fill. Cards, sheets and menus are never frosted.

## Shapes

Three shapes carry the system: the 20 px card, the pill and the rounded rectangle. Cards, and the docked side panel, use 20 px corners (r071; they were 12). The pill (999 px, or `--lm-key-radius` at 24 px, which is a full pill at 32 to 48 px) is the gold primary key and the Run key, chips (a 22 px tag, a 28 px acting chip), the round targets of icon keys and status icons, grade badges, switches, meter tracks and dots. Every other control is a rounded rectangle in the card's language: secondary keys, fields, steppers and slider wells use 10 px (8 px for small keys, 12 px for large ones), the segmented track 12 px with 9 px segments 3 px inside it, banners, the date row and the sidebar's rows 14 px (`--lm-radius-field`), menus, popovers, toasts and the agent banner 12 px; tooltips, small marks, day cells and menu rows 8 px; checkboxes and tiny marks 4 px. Dialogs and the top corners of bottom sheets use 24 px (r071; they were 18). The navigation pill and the docked flow bar's control pill use 36 px ends on a 64 px body, with 28 px (phone) or circular (desktop) pressed states inside the navigation. Vertical segment stacks, and the two-column banks a narrow sheet folds a four- or five-key bank into, round to 24 px. The setup progress row is a line of 4 px pills. Edges are 0.5 px gold hairlines on containers and 1 px on controls; dashed lines appear only where they mean something (the D grade, the Ideal rung, composite series).

Icons are line icons (Lucide plus an authored set) at 1.5 px stroke with round caps and joins, 20 to 24 px. Status is a 6 px dot; a stale result is a 6 px gold dot.

## Components

### Keys (buttons)
The primary key is a gold pill; every other key is a rounded rectangle in the card's language. No bevel and no shadow; tactile through brightness and a small scale, not depth.
- **Shape:** 40 px tall by default, 32 px small, 48 px large; icon-only keys are round at the same heights. Touch targets extend invisibly to 44 px (48 px for icon keys, 32 px under a fine pointer).
- **Primary (the gold pill):** Ember Gold fill, near-black label at 15/600 (12/600 on the small key), 22 px side padding (14 px small), the corner `--lm-key-radius` (24 px: a full pill at 32, 40 and 48 px, and not a stadium when a full-width key's label wraps to two lines). An optional arrow after the label (`arrow`, or `trailingIcon`): an 18 px icon at stroke 2.25, never the glyph, because the arrow U+2192 is in neither Geist file; a trailing icon takes 4 px less padding on its side. Hover lifts it to Lit Gold; press scales it to .98 and dims it to brightness 0.94; focus is the global 2 px gold ring. One per view.
- **Action:** gold label on a 16 % gold tile, 32 px tall (28 small, 40 large), 8 px corners: the inline verb on a row ("Log").
- **Secondary (outlined, the default):** the neutral tile: 6 % ivory fill, 1 px strong hairline, ivory label, 10 px corners (8 small, 12 large); hover takes the fill to 11 %.
- **Text:** gold label, no border, 10 px padding. Icon-only text keys are stone icons that brighten to ivory over a 6 % ivory hover.
- **Danger:** muted-rose label, a 45 % rose outline and a 6 % rose tint.
- **Pressed / toggled:** the selected-segment treatment: gold wash, gold edge, ivory label.
- **Disabled:** a primary becomes a Soft Charcoal pill with a pale-stone label (never a half-faded gold); the other keys keep their own shape at 42 %. Disabled keys stay focusable when they carry a reason.
- **Run key:** the Simulator and Planner primary: the same gold pill shape (a full pill, 40 px, 48 large, label 14/600) that shows a 6 px near-black dot when results are stale and a 14 px spinning arc plus elapsed time while running. On Simulate it never sits in the header (the title row is the scenario switcher and the ⋯): it ends the schedule's horizon row, and on Results it heads the stage beside the horizon ("84 days · 5 Oct → 27 Dec"), or is the empty card's action before the first run. One Run per screen state.
- **Motion:** colour and border changes over 140 ms expo-out; brightness and scale on press over 80 ms.

### Segmented control (Key Bank, Link Bank, Tabs)
- **Track:** a rounded-rectangle well (Deep Charcoal, a 0.5 px hairline, 12 px corners: `--lm-radius-track`) with 3 px padding and gap; the segments sit inside it with 9 px corners, concentric with the track. Two to six segments (more becomes a select).
- **One row, sized to the labels:** every segment is one grid column, at least as wide as its own label and never narrower than 48 px (40 px in the small bank), sharing any spare width equally. A track sized to its content makes all segments as wide as the longest label; a stretched (block) track gives each an equal share; a squeezed track lets short labels give their spare room to a long one ("Yes · No · Prefer not to say" keeps the long label on one line). Only when the labels themselves cannot fit does the track scroll sideways, with no visible bar, keeping the selected segment in view. A label never wraps and a segment never drops to a second row.
- **Sizes:** default 36 px tall, 14 px side padding; small 28 px, 10 px padding; large 44 px, 16 px padding. Under a coarse pointer every segment is at least 44 px tall, small banks included (r071; they were 36 and 28 on touch). In the header's title row or the action row the track takes the bar height (36 / 44 px) and the segments fill it. In a sheet the segments tighten to 8 px side padding so three fit a 360 px phone, and below a 520 px sheet a four- or five-key bank folds into two equal columns (an odd last key spans the row).
- **Labels (r071):** mono caps at every size: Geist Mono 500, UPPERCASE, +0.08em, 11 px (12 px from 1024), the meta role; a label is a fact about the choice. Icons 18 px (16 px small).
- **Segment:** pale stone; hover to ivory over a 4 % tint, only where a pointer hovers.
- **Selected:** gold wash fill, gold edge, ivory label. No dots or underlines. A 6 px gold dot after a label marks changed results.

### Chips and status icons
- **Chip:** a pill (r071). Read-only it is a 22 px tag: a 6 % ivory tint, a stone label-s, no border. When it acts (a filter, an `onClick`, a link) it is a 28 px pill with a 1 px hairline, and it grows to the 48 px touch target under a coarse pointer below 768 px. Filter chips read in stone and take the gold-wash selected treatment. Status chips are a 6 px dot in the severity colour and the words, nothing around them. A domain chip (`kind="domain"`) carries a 6 px dot in the domain's solid (`domainColor`); its words stay stone.
- **Status icon** (`StatusCapsule`): the header's status, as an icon button with no words: a 48 px round target, no fill and no border, holding one 22 px Lucide line icon in stone (1.5 px stroke); hover takes a 6 % ivory circle and ivory icon, like the bar's other keys. While the state wants the person (a check-in due, changes or proposals waiting, questions asked later, a stop) a 6 px gold dot sits at the icon's top right (rose for a stop such as a sync error); it breathes (opacity 1 to 0.35 over 1100 ms) only for real activity, like a sync in flight or a long search. Its accessible name carries the full meaning ("Weekly check-in ready", "2 changes waiting"), and the same words show as a tooltip on hover, keyboard focus and a 500 ms long-press on touch (the held press then does not activate it). A press does exactly what the state's place does: the check-in sheet, the changes sheet, the paused sheet, Your body's setup, the safety-mode popover, Plan details, Settings › Sync; a status with nothing to open shows its tooltip instead.
- **The icons:** check-in ClipboardCheck; changes or proposals waiting Inbox; paused CirclePause; body not set up UserRound; safety mode ShieldCheck; questions asked later MessageCircleQuestionMark; results out of date History; a long search ScanSearch; the running plan Route (on planning screens; it opens Plan details); the Coach offline WifiOff and busy Hourglass; sync CloudCheck, RefreshCw while syncing, CloudOff offline, CloudAlert when it stopped.
- **At most two:** a bar shows at most two status icons (one beside a primary key on a phone, whose row has no room for two), the page's own and the shell's (the plan's, sync's) counted together: the ones that want attention first, then the page's order, then the plan's, then sync's. The rest move to the top of the ⋯ as items that do what the icon does (one with nothing to open is listed as its words), before the page's own items; on desktop this brings the ⋯ even on a page without actions. ### Cards (Faceplate)

- **Background:** the grained card material (Charcoal Card at 83 % over the grain, with the 3.5 % gold wash). - **Actions and states live in the head:** a card's own verb ("Add a goal", "Edit pantry", "Mark all as planned") and its state ("All done" as a status chip) sit at the head's right, never floating under a list and never as a card of their own holding only a word. ### Domain card (r071)
A card says what it shows with its domain, not how much of it there is. `data-domain="energy"` (also `heart`, `steps`, `food`, `recovery`, `body`, `sleep`, `videos`; `plan` or `gold` for the Next-up card; `neutral` or no attribute is the default card) on a `Faceplate`, `.lm-material` or `.lm-card` paints one soft glow in that hue and sets four variables its marks read: `--lm-hue` (the solid: icons, today's bar, in-range ticks, status dots), `--lm-ink-h` (the soft tint for units, mono meta and the footer state word), `--lm-deep` (the glow core: never a mark or text) and `--lm-dim` (the unlit remainder of a track). Text never wears the solid; focal marks on the glow are ivory. The glow never moves or scales with the data. Each domain has its own position, size and core strength (`--lm-glow-<domain>-pos`, `-size`, `-a`), chosen so that small text and solid marks sit outside the core. One hero number per card, with its unit small and muted beside it, and facts in mono caps. The recipe, with its rules, is `design/COMPONENTS.md` §16.

### Dialogs, sheets, side panels
One header grammar decides where an overlay's way out and its commit live, and a ✕ never sits beside a Cancel.
- **Sheet and side-panel header** (r068 L1/L19; one 56 px row, the title left-aligned on the gutter in title-m like every page title): when the panel commits something it reads **title · Cancel**, and the commit is a real filled primary key, verb first ("Save", "Log", "Pause", "Find plans"): full width (48 px) at the foot of a phone sheet, where the thumb is; a small filled key after Cancel in a desktop side panel's head. Cancel is a stone text key (label 14/20) with a 48 px invisible target and a 6 % ivory hover. With nothing to commit (read-only, or every choice applies at once) the head reads **title · ✕**. A commit that cannot be used yet stays focusable in the disabled key look and carries the reason as its tooltip ("Type your weight first."); a commit bound to a form submits it, so Enter in any field commits too. While a desktop side panel is open the page reflows beside it; nothing lies under it.
- **Dialog:** for destructive confirmation, import preview and first-run consent only. Raised Charcoal, 0.5 px hairline, 24 px corners (r071; they were 18), 480 px wide (640 px wide variant), a 24/30 title at 600 with 24 px insets, stone 14/21 body. A dialog that asks for a decision ends in **Cancel · verb** at the foot, right-aligned gold text keys with 12 px padding and an 8 % gold hover (danger stays rose; the verb names the act, never "OK"), and has no ✕. A read-only dialog has no footer keys and shows **title · ✕**. The first-run consent has neither ✕ nor Esc. Enters from 6 px below at 98.5 % scale over 200 ms over the 60 % scrim; under reduced motion it does not move and cross-fades in 100 ms.
- **Sheet:** the phone editor. Deep Charcoal, 24 px top corners (r071; they were 18), a 0.5 px top hairline, a 32 × 4 grab handle (stone at 40 %) in a 22 px strip. It opens at its content's height (up to 92 %), with 35 / 60 / 92 % detents where a sheet asks for them; a swipe steps down or closes. It opens on a spring (320 ms with one damped overshoot of 2.5 %, `--lm-ease-spring`, behind `@supports (transition-timing-function: linear(0, 1))`, the sheet curve elsewhere), leaves in 220 ms with an ease-in, and the scrim fades in over 200 ms; a hard, unblurred colour extension below the screen edge hides the gap the overshoot would open (offscreen at rest, so it is not a shadow). Under reduced motion, from the OS or from Settings › Motion, a sheet does not move: it cross-fades in 100 ms. The body scrolls under a fixed header with a 16 px inset (20 px at the end) and 16 px between blocks. A sticky footer exists only for stepped flows or secondary keys, never for the commit.
- **Log sheets** commit from the panel's own commit key, never from a body button: "Log a fast" is title · Cancel with Log at the foot, with Started and Ended fields and the length as a large readout under a "Length" label ("16 h 30"); the verb carries the field's error as its tooltip while the times are invalid.
- **Side panel:** the same header and materials at 392 px from 1024 px; docked in the flow (from 1280 px) it takes the card material and its 20 px corner. An overlay panel springs in from the right as a sheet does from the bottom (320 ms, one 2.5 % overshoot, out in 220 ms, a colour extension past the right edge hiding the overshoot gap) and cross-fades in 100 ms under reduced motion. The page reflows beside an overlay panel and stays live; a reading or editing panel over prose and cards may opt into the 60 % scrim, which a tap closes.

**The One Way Out Rule.** An overlay draws exactly one way out: Cancel when it commits, ✕ when it does not. The commit is the filled primary key the panel draws (a phone sheet's foot, a side panel's head), or the verb at a dialog's foot, and never a key in a sheet's body.

**The Focus Return Rule (r071).** When a phone Sheet, a SidePanel or a Dialog closes, focus returns to the control that opened it (`useRestoreFocus`): the opener is captured once per opening, the restore retries for 1.5 s while the page is still inert behind the closing dialog, a host that closes by unmounting still gets its focus back, an opener that was replaced while the overlay was open is found again by its id or by its parent, and a sheet that closes as the next one opens (a program's sheet whose action opens the day editor) hands its opener to it, so closing the second goes back to the first's opener. Without it a keyboard user is left on `<body>` (audit B P1-1).

- **Header:** title · ✕, the meal's name as the title. An answer applies at once, so the sheet commits nothing of its own.
- **Fact line** (stone, 14/20): before the meal is logged, its targets as a readout ("energy · protein · carbs · fat"; example foods in quiet mode); after, what was logged against the plan ("Logged 430 kcal · planned 1 332 kcal") or the answer word.
- **How it went** (today and past days only): As planned · Partly · Skipped as one large block bank, three equal 48 px segments on one row, the current answer selected. Choosing an answer saves it, closes the sheet and toasts with Undo; choosing the current answer again only closes the sheet.
- **Then text keys** in gold, flush with the text (no side padding) and 20 px apart: "I ate something else" ("Add more" once logged) and "Recipe and targets". When recipes cannot be made for the day, one stone line names the reason with the one text key that fixes it.
- No full-width key and no second card in the body.

### Navigation
- **Style:** a floating dock pill (Charcoal Card at 94 %, `--lm-nav-pill-bg`, a 0.5 px hairline, 36 px ends, 64 px tall) of five labelled keys that share its width equally, each about 56 px tall: a 22 px icon over a 12 px label. The accessible name is the destination's name.
- **States:** inactive keys in pale stone. The current key is an IVORY capsule with a near-black icon (stroke 2) and a 600 label: ivory marks where you are, and gold stays for what you can do or chose. ONE thumb draws the capsule and glides to the pressed key by transform in 280 ms with the one damped overshoot (`--lm-ease-needle`); it jumps under reduced motion, a page the dock does not list shows no capsule, and a mode change cross-fades the keys. Forced colours keep a filled capsule in the system's Highlight pair. A press shrinks the key's icon and label to .96 for 80 ms. A 6 px gold dot at the icon's top right marks a waiting proposal or stale results; on the current key it is near-black with a capsule-coloured ring, because gold on ivory would be 1.5:1. (Replaces r068's "the current destination's icon turns gold, with no fill, underline or label" and the icon-only pill.)
- **Mobile treatment:** centred in a tintless frosted dock across the bottom edge (4 px above and below the pill, 10 px at the sides, safe areas added), hidden while the on-screen keyboard is up and absent on pushed and setup screens. Below 1024 px only: from 1024 px the sidebar is the navigation.

### Sidebar
The desktop navigation from 1024 px, on the website and in the desktop app alike. Not shown below 1024 px.
- **Material:** full height and sticky at the left, bg-2 at 83 % over the grain, a 0.5 px hairline on its right edge.
- **Expanded (240 px):** the plan-ring mark (22 px, see Brand mark) and "Vitals" (title-m) at the top, level with the top bar's keys; the mode's destinations as 40 px rows with 14 px corners, a 20 px icon (pale stone) and the label (14/20 at 500, stone). The current row takes a 9 % ivory tint, an ivory icon and a 600 ivory label (r071: ivory is where you are; no gold and no side stripe; forced colours draw it as a 2 px Highlight outline); hover takes a 6 % ivory tint. At the foot: Living has Planning tools, Evidence and Settings; the planning override has Back to Today, Coach and Settings; planning without a plan has Coach and Settings.
- **Collapsed (64 px):** the same rows as icons only. The labels fade and clip, each row's name shows in a tooltip to its right, and accessible names do not change. The icons keep the same position in both states.
- **Toggle:** the "Sidebar" key at the far left of the top bar (aria-expanded) and Ctrl/⌘ + B. The width eases over 200 ms, instantly under reduced motion. The choice is kept per device and never synced.
- A 6 px gold dot at an icon's top right marks a waiting proposal or stale results, as on the pill.

### Docked flow bar
A focused flow's step keys (Back · Skip · Next) on a phone, in the navigation pill's place and built like it. Below 1024 px only; from 1024 px the same keys are a plain row after the content.
- **Pill:** the navigation pill's material and shape: Charcoal Card at 94 %, 0.5 px hairline, 36 px ends, 64 px tall (it grows if the row must wrap), full width up to 34 rem, centred in the dock's insets. The keys always sit on this solid pill, so nothing scrolling underneath reads through them.
- **Keys:** at the bar height (36 / 44 px), inset by half the difference to the pill (14 / 10 px) so a gold pill key at the end sits concentric with the pill's round end. One line: Back (text) at the start, Skip or Cancel (text) and Next (the gold primary pill) at the end; a lone key spans the pill.
- **Frost and room:** the shared three-layer tintless frost (4 / 9 / 14 px, separately masked) under the bar, fading in over 24 px above it, no fill and no line. The bar is sticky at the bottom of the column, takes its own room (no empty gap after it) and lifts toasts above itself.
- **Welcome steps:** the first-run welcome uses the same pill as its sticky foot at every width, with 8 px padding, the one primary key filling it and an optional short stone note (12/18) beside it.

### Setup progress row
The one progress indicator of the first-run steps and the intake chapters: its own row inside the header, under Back and the centred step title.
- **Segments:** one 4 px pill per step or chapter, 6 px apart, sharing the width equally; full width on phones, a centred 420 px row from 768 px.
- **States, whole only:** the step you are on is Ember Gold, a finished step is Step Bronze, a later or unfinished step is the bare Soft Charcoal track. No partial fill, no count, no names, no dashed or hatched state; a change of state cross-fades over 200 ms. The summary has no current step and shows the finished ones filled.
- **Announced, not printed:** the row is a progress bar whose value text carries the chapter and "question 4 of 11"; the welcome steps are an ordered list whose items announce their state.

### Brand mark (the plan ring)
One mark everywhere: launcher, favicon, PWA, desktop and tray icons, the Android splash and notification icon, the sidebar, the welcome page and the start screen. It belongs to the sister app Gauge's reading-ring family (a thin ring on Ember Black, Ember Gold and Ivory, round caps, the same 24-unit geometry, weights and safe zones) with the colour roles swapped, so the two never read alike side by side. **Gauge measures:** a gold reading arc over 70 % of a hairline bezel from 12 o'clock, with an ivory dot where the reading stops. **Vitals plans:** an ivory ring drawn almost all the way round, left open at four o'clock where the next part of the plan has not been lived yet (only the hairline bezel crosses it), and a gold dot inside the ring aimed at that opening: the next thing to do, the same gold as Next up.
- **Geometry** (24-unit design space, centre 12,12, outer edge 9.95), shared with Gauge through `design/brand/mark-geometry.json` (the same file is in the Gauge repo): every line and circle has the same weight and size in both marks. Standard: stroke 1.2 (ring radius 9.35), opening 34° centred on 35° (four o'clock); bezel the full circle at 0.5 and 36 % ink; dot r 1.5 on the opening's bisector, 5.3 from the centre (Gauge's end dot is the same r 1.5).
- **Cuts by rendered size** (the same tiers and boosts as Gauge): above 40 px the standard cut; 25–40 px a medium cut (stroke 1.6, bezel 0.6, dot r 1.75, opening 40°); 24 px and under the small cut (stroke 2.0, dot r 2.0, opening 46°, no bezel), so it stays an open ring with a gold dot at 16 px. The themed and monochrome icons use a one-colour cut (stroke 1.4, bezel 0.35 at 50 %, dot r 1.7); the notification icon its own (ring edge 9.4, stroke 2.0, bezel 0.5 at 50 %, dot r 1.6), as Gauge's does.
- **Placement:** Android layers at 3× (outer edge 29.85 dp, inside the 33 dp safe radius; the splash inside its 192 dp circle); notification icon 24 dp at 1×, white, 2.6 dp padding (Gauge's end dot takes it to 2 dp); rounded Ember tiles with the outer edge at 0.37 of the side (0.4375 at 48 px and under); PWA maskable and monochrome at 0.34, inside the 40 % safe circle; tray glyphs at 0.45 without a tile. The in-app mark crops to `2 2 20 20`, so its size is the visible ring (22 px in the sidebar and the welcome header, 88 px on the start screen).
- **Motion:** the start screen and the Android splash keep the bezel still, draw the ring round it from 5 % in 500 ms (fast-out-slow-in), then pop the dot from 420 ms over 220 ms with one damped overshoot; reduced motion shows the still mark.
- **Source:** `design/brand/vitals-mark.py` reads `design/brand/mark-geometry.json` and writes every asset and the in-app geometry (`src/components/brand/mark-geometry.ts`); change a weight or size in the JSON, copy it to Gauge's `docs/design-concepts/`, and run both generators; never edit a generated file by hand.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** build every surface from the tokens (`var(--lm-*)` or the Tailwind roles mapped from them); edit `design/tokens.css` and copy it to `src/styles/tokens.css`.
- **Do** give cards the grained card material, a 0.5 px gold hairline and 20 px corners, with 12 px between cards (16 px from 1024 px) and 18 px (24 px from 1024 px) inside; opt a card into its domain glow with `data-domain`, and nowhere else.
- **Do** make the primary key a gold pill and every other control a rounded rectangle in the card's language, and size every control in the header row and the action row to the bar height (36 px fine pointer, 44 px coarse).
- **Do** keep gold for the action, the selection and the moment, and ivory for where you are; selected things take the gold wash, the 40 % gold edge and ivory text, and data wears its domain hue.
- **Do** set numbers tabular with the unit inline, smaller and in stone (hero, wide and tile numbers are light and proportional), and give every chart series, severity and grade a label, icon, pip or dash in addition to its colour.
- **Do** use 400 for body, 500 for labels, keys and mono meta, 600 for titles and values, and 300 only for numerals of 36 px and up.
- **Do** set facts in mono caps (a date, a range, a unit, a state, a legend label, a time, a tick) and everything else in sentence case.
- **Do** let motion report state: 140 ms for hover and selection, 200 ms for panels, a 320 ms spring in and 220 ms out for sheets and side panels, 280 ms with one damped overshoot for the dock's thumb, 420 ms for needles and readouts; count a number up, sweep a gauge, draw a line and grow bars once, when 20 % in view; and render the final state at once under reduced motion, with overlays cross-fading in 100 ms.
- **Do** give every sheet and side panel the one grammar: title · Cancel with a filled commit key (foot on phones, head on desktop) when it commits, title · ✕ when it does not. A dialog that asks for a decision ends in Cancel · verb at its foot and shows no ✕.
- **Do** open a tapped object row's sheet (title · ✕, one fact line, the answer bank, then text keys) and reuse the one meal sheet wherever a meal row appears.
- **Do** keep a key bank on one row: segments at least their label's width (48 px minimum), the bar height inside a bar, scrolling only when the labels themselves cannot fit.
- **Do** dock a phone flow's step keys on the navigation pill's material in the pill's place, at the bar height, when the screen has no navigation pill.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add shadows, bevels or blur to cards, sheets or menus; depth is tone, hairline and grain. A card's one domain glow is the only glow, and only inside the card.
- **Don't** put grain or gradients on anything except the page backdrop, the card material (its wash, or its one domain glow) and (grain only) the desktop sidebar; never two gradient layers on one card, never a glow behind small text, never an animated gradient.
- **Don't** use gold for body text, decoration, a data series or a fill stronger than an 18 % wash.
- **Don't** nest a card inside a card.
- **Don't** add a second top bar, a second ⋯, a persistent bottom bar or a bar above the navigation pill; at most one banner, directly under the top bar.
- **Don't** write eyebrows, kickers or explanatory subtitles; a line beside a heading states a fact or does not exist.
- **Don't** set text in uppercase except the mono caps role for facts, don't put a mono line above a heading as a kicker (the top bar's date line is the one exception), and don't use a weight above 600 or a light weight under 36 px.
- **Don't** colour banners, headings or side stripes by severity; colour sits on the mark only.
- **Don't** add a light theme, a theme switch or a `prefers-color-scheme` branch; Vitals is dark only.
- **Don't** generate new data hues; a ninth series reuses a category hue with a dash pattern and marker shape.
- **Don't** plot the eight domain hues together (all pairs fail the colour-vision gates; wheel neighbours only) or let a domain solid be a text colour. (This replaces the r068 Don't "put a cool hue in any data palette".)
- **Don't** put a ✕ beside a Cancel, a commit key in a sheet's body or footer, or a full-width Save anywhere in a sheet.
- **Don't** put words in the header's status or show more than two status icons; the words are the icon's name and tooltip, and the rest go into the ⋯.
- **Don't** wrap a segment label, drop a segment to a second row or shrink a segment below its label.
- **Don't** fill the setup progress row partially, number it, name its segments or dash them; a step is current, finished or not yet.
- **Don't** float the docked flow bar's keys on frost alone; they always sit on the solid pill.
